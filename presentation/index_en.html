Challenge #645

Little d - Big trouble

The FIPS 186-5 standard requires that during RSA key generation, the private exponent d must be greater than 2**(size//2). The standard also specifies that in the extremely rare case where d is smaller, new prime numbers p and q must be generated.

Try your luck by submitting one of these extremely rare cases.
